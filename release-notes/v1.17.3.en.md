# v1.17.3 — Volume Thresholds for Scene Detection (fixes "video detected while nobody watches")

## Root cause (caught by user testing)

With nobody watching, overview/smart still showed "video/video call". Two holes:
1. **Platform-domain hits had no volume gate** — a standby TV/app touching a streaming domain (thumbnail preload, a few KB) scored full marks; WeChat ICE keepalives (UDP 3478, a few hundred bytes) counted as meetings.
2. **The "heavy downlink" bulk evidence didn't separate downloads from video** — a background Baidu-netdisk download (83MB) on the NAS read as streaming.

## Fix (prefer misses over false positives: a miss just shows "browsing", a false positive destroys trust)

- Platform-domain connections <32KB = keepalive heartbeat, not scene evidence (applies to gaming/work/streaming)
- Meeting domains / STUN need ≥64KB bidirectional cumulative before counting as a call
- Streaming domains need real downlink ≥512KB (thumbnail/preload is not watching)
- Netdisk download CDN domains (baidupcs/bcsp2p, confirmed live) → download scene, no longer mixed into streaming
